## Coverage limitations reported by failed jobs

- `twenty-thousand-paragraphs`, `quarto-cli-playwright-html-color-scheme-scroll-position`, and `parsing-very-long-line` reported pages too large for screenshots. After a full-page screenshot attempt fails, `verify/capture.mjs` returns a limit marker above 20 million pixels instead of tiling. `verify/compare.mjs` accepts matching limit markers with equal dimensions; that establishes matching dimensions, **not pixel equality**. Design a bounded comparison that actually covers pixels, or explicitly surface this as incomplete coverage; retain mismatch detection and resource limits. The list is from failed-job logs; inspect successful-job reports too before claiming the complete warning inventory.
- Logs also report forced-state targets as unreachable, including clipped Quarto skip links and non-focusable `main` elements, on original and optimized pages. These are coverage warnings rather than the incomplete reachable-target failures above. Check whether each classification is valid and whether keyboard-accessible states remain covered. Full per-document warnings are in the run reports.
